Find the Best Bloodborne Armor

This Bloodborne armor calculator searches the complete four-slot attire space to find a strong set for Physical, Blunt, Thrust, Blood, Arcane, Fire, Bolt, poison, Frenzy or Beasthood resistance. Choose the stat that matters most, set a tiebreaker and decide how much of the primary resistance you are willing to trade for a better overall total.

How the Armor Optimizer Works

Scenario A maximizes the selected primary resistance. Scenario B finds the highest weighted total among combinations that remain within your tolerance of Scenario A. The optimizer evaluates 125 attire pieces across every Hat, Garb, Glove and Trouser combination, using the same calculation model as the original Excel/VBA ledger.

Bloodborne Resistance Calculator

The tool runs entirely in your browser, with no account or backend required. Results show each selected item, the primary resistance total, the tiebreaker, the weighted total and the trade between the two recommended sets. For a different priority, change the controls and run the optimizer again.
